Reports a Suite Setup, Suite Teardown, Test Setup, Test Teardown, Task Setup or Task Teardown setting in the *** Settings *** section that does not call a keyword.

Such a setting has no effect because no keyword is executed for it, so it is effectively dead configuration. It is usually left over after the referenced keyword call was removed or was never filled in.

Example:


*** Settings ***
Suite Setup    # nothing follows -> will be marked as empty
Test Teardown    Close All Browsers

Text after this comment will only be shown in the settings of the inspection.

Removing the empty setting keeps the Settings section concise and avoids confusion about whether a suite- or test-level setup or teardown is actually configured.
